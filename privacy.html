Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains how emmyluna.be (the “Website”) and the Emmy Luna Social Stats integration (the “App”) handle personal data. The App connects to the Instagram API and the TikTok API to show statistics from Emmy Luna's own social media accounts on the Website.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Emmy Luna, content creator, based in Belgium.
E-mail: emmy-luna@hotmail.com · Phone: +32 488 45 87 18

The Instagram and TikTok integration

Whose data is accessed

The App is used only by Emmy Luna to connect her own Instagram account (@emmy.luna) and TikTok account (@emmyluna8). It is not offered to other users and does not access the accounts of visitors, followers or other creators.

What data is accessed

  • Instagram: basic profile information (username, account ID, follower count, media count) and account-level insights (such as views, reach, interactions and aggregated, anonymous audience demographics by age range, gender and country).
  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name, open ID), profile statistics (follower count, like count, video count) and public statistics of her own videos (view, like, comment and share counts).

The App does not read private messages, does not post, comment or like anything, and does not collect personal data about individual followers or viewers. Audience data is only received in aggregated, anonymous form.

How the data is used

The data is used only to calculate and display up-to-date totals (for example follower count, views and engagement rate) in the statistics section of the Website, so brands can see current account performance. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models.

Storage and security

Access tokens and the latest statistics are stored in encrypted storage on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers KV). Tokens are never shown on the Website or sent to visitors' browsers. Only the aggregated statistics are published. Statistics are refreshed a few times per day and older values are overwritten.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time:

  • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions / Apps and websites → remove “Emmy Luna Social Stats”.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services → remove “Emmy Luna Social Stats”.

To request deletion of all stored tokens and statistics, e-mail emmy-luna@hotmail.com with the subject “Data deletion”. All stored data is deleted within 30 days, and you will receive a confirmation.

Visitors of the Website

  • No analytics or tracking. The Website does not use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies.
  • Language preference. Your chosen language (NL/EN) is saved in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and can be cleared in your browser settings.
  • Contact form. The contact form opens your own e-mail program. Nothing is stored by the Website. E-mails you send are kept only as long as needed to reply and handle a collaboration.
  • Third-party services. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the Website and statistics service are delivered through hosting and CDN providers, including Cloudflare. These providers receive your IP address as part of normal web traffic.

Legal basis

Data is processed on the basis of legitimate interest (presenting the creator's own account statistics to potential partners) and, for the Instagram and TikTok connection, the account owner's consent given when authorizing the App.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. Contact emmy-luna@hotmail.com to use these rights. You can also file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

Changes

This policy may be updated when the Website or App changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
